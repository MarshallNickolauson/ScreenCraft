Roadmap

What we plan to build next. Nothing here is a promise or a date; it is the list we are working through, roughly in order. Follow progress in the changelog or on GitHub.

Next

  • Windows polish. Platform Test and harden the Windows build end to end: capture, audio, the Media Foundation H.264 path, camera and the installer.
  • Linux recording and editing. Platform Linux has no operating-system H.264, so recordings cannot be edited there yet. We are designing a path that stays free of bundled H.264 code.
  • HEVC export. Export Bring back HEVC for exports and the compressor, through the operating system's own encoders.
  • AAC audio in older projects. Projects Open the AAC audio in 0.3 recordings and in compressor inputs.

Recording

  • Menu-bar and tray icon. Start, stop and see elapsed time without the floating bar.
  • Live system-audio meter before you press record.
  • Bluetooth headphone guard. Keep headphones in stereo while the microphone is on, as 0.3 did.
  • Recording HUD placed beside the area you are capturing.

Editing

  • More device frames and finer control over their colour and bezels.
  • Caption styles. More presets, per-word animation and caption translation on your own machine.
  • Smarter auto-zoom. Better detection of what you are working on, with easy manual correction.
  • Cursor styles. A wider set of pointers and click effects.
  • Undo history panel to step back through any edit.

Export

  • Export presets for common destinations: social, slides and docs.
  • Batch export of several projects in one go.
  • Faster rendering through further GPU work in the compositor.

Distribution

  • Signed and notarized builds so macOS and Windows stop warning on first launch.
  • Linux packages (AppImage and Flatpak) once editing works there.

Missing something you need? Open an issue and tell us.
