Camera layouts

The camera is recorded as its own track, so you decide how it appears after the fact.

Use your phone as the camera

Any phone that appears as a camera on your computer works, and it is listed in the recorder's camera menu like any other camera (marked as a phone camera). Plug it in by USB first, then open the menu; it looks again every time it opens.

  • iPhone on a Mac: unlock it and plug it in. It shows up through Continuity Camera (macOS Ventura or later; the Mac and iPhone are normally signed in to the same Apple ID).
  • Android (Android 14 or later): plug it in, open the USB notification and choose Webcam. Older phones can use an app such as Camo or DroidCam, which adds a virtual camera.

Choose Camera in the recorder and switch on 9:16 to record a vertical talking-head video straight from the phone.

Layouts

LayoutWhat it does
BubbleA small camera in a corner of the frame.
LargeA bigger bubble, for moments when you are talking.
Camera onlyThe camera fills the frame and the screen fades out.
Side by sideScreen and camera next to each other, camera on the right or left.
HiddenCamera off for that stretch.

The camera track

Layouts live on the Camera track. Add a block at the playhead, drag its ends to change the length, and pick its layout. Where blocks meet, the change eases over about half a second.

Per-block settings

Every block can override the project's camera look: size, roundness, shape (square, 4:3, 16:9, 3:4), position on a nine-point grid, mirroring, border and shadow. Position can sit flush against the frame edge.

While zoomed

The bubble scales down as the screen zooms in so it stays out of the way. The amount is set with "Scale while zoomed".
