Pace and transitions

Keep the interesting parts at full speed and move quickly through the rest.

Speed per clip

Select a clip and set its speed. Sped-up clips show one compact marker (for example 2x) instead of a row of slivers.

Jump cuts

In the Pace panel (or with Auto jump cuts above the timeline), ScreenCraft listens to your microphone and finds the pauses in your speech. It does not delete them and does not speed them up: each pause becomes a greyed-out jump cut on the Video track. Playback skips it and the export leaves it out, but it is still there to see and change. Set how long a silence has to be, how much to keep around your words and how sensitive the detection is, then press Find jump cuts.

To adjust one, drag its edges (the clip beside it gives or takes the time). Select any clip and press X to switch it off as a jump cut, or press X on a jump cut to turn it back on. To cut your own section, split the clip with S at both ends and press X on the middle piece. Turn all jump cuts off brings everything back.

Find idle stretches

In the Pace panel, choose a minimum length and let ScreenCraft find stretches with no clicks, typing or cursor motion. Optionally keep the parts where you are speaking. Apply a speed to all of them in one step.

Ramps

A speed ramp eases from one speed to another instead of jumping, and appears on the timeline as one block (for example 2.3x to 4.2x).

Freeze frames

Hold the current frame for a set time, useful for pointing something out in a still.

Transitions

Where two clips meet you can add a cross-dissolve, fade, flash, blur, slide or zoom. Dissolves use footage from both sides of the cut, so nothing overlaps or loses time.
