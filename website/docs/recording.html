Recording

Open ScreenCraft to get the floating recorder bar. Choose a source (display, window or dragged area), toggle camera, microphone and system audio, and press Record. A short countdown starts the recording and can be cancelled. Press the stop button or the global shortcut to finish; the editor opens automatically.

What is captured

  • The screen video, without the system cursor.
  • Cursor position, clicks and keyboard shortcuts in a separate log, so the cursor can be restyled in the editor.
  • Optional camera and microphone as separate tracks.

Plain typing is never stored, only shortcuts and special keys, used for the on-screen shortcut display.

Talking-head videos (no screen)

Choose Camera in the Capture group to record just the camera, with no screen at all. The camera is the picture, so every editing tool still works: jump cuts, captions, zooms, music, sound levels and export. Switch on 9:16 to record vertically, like a phone video: a landscape camera is cropped to 9:16 around its centre, and a phone held upright is used as it is. When the recording ends, the editor opens with the pauses in your speech already switched off as jump cuts.

Vertical areas

With Area chosen, the 9:16 switch keeps the area you draw tall and narrow, for screen recordings made for a phone.

Long recordings

When you stop, ScreenCraft builds a lightweight preview in the background so editing stays smooth even for recordings of an hour or more. Your original footage is never modified.
